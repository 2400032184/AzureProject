Vending Workflow

1. Request
Application owner submits business and technical details.
2. Validate
Check naming, owner, cost center, environment and region.
3. Approve
Authorized reviewer approves the request.
4. Vend
Automation prepares subscription configuration and landing zone.
5. Govern
Management group, Policy, RBAC, tags and budget are applied.
6. Handoff
Application team receives controlled access.
